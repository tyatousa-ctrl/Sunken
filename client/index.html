Sunken Sicily

Co-op VR scuba treasure hunt · intro sequence

This device can't do VR. To play, open this link in the Meta Quest Browser on your headset. You can still look around here.

Controls
  • On deck: left stick walks, right stick snap-turns, A/X jumps, grip grabs
  • Blunderbuss: trigger fires; after two shots flick your wrist down then up to reload (or A/X). Grip the barrel with your other hand to aim two-handed
  • Scuba gear: tank: let go of it over your shoulder · mask: hold it to your face · fins: grab them
  • Swim: hold grip and pull your arms through the water
  • Bubble jets: trigger shoots bubbles from that hand and pushes you the other way; both hands for a boost, opposite directions to spin
  • Underwater sticks: left drifts where you look · right turns, up/down to rise and sink
  • Air: watch your left wrist; refill in the bubbling vent or grab the yellow tank
  • Desktop: drag to look, WASD move, Space jump/rise, Q sink, Shift jets; your hand: E grab/drop, F trigger, R reload
