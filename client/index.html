Sunken Sicily

Co-op VR scuba treasure hunt · Level 1

Crew

Playing solo. Create a crew and share its code, or join one.

This device can't do VR. To play, open this link in the Meta Quest Browser on your headset. You can still look around here.

Controls
  • On deck: left stick walks, right stick snap-turns, A/X jumps, grip grabs
  • Grab from a distance: point at anything you can pick up, up to 5 ft away (it lights up and the pointer turns gold), and grip: it flies to your hand. Let go before it arrives to send it back
  • Blunderbuss: trigger fires; after two shots flick your wrist down then up to reload (or A/X). Grip the barrel with your other hand to aim two-handed
  • Beer: hold a mug under the tap and pull the trigger; raise it to your mouth and tilt to drink (too many and you'll pass out)
  • Darts: grab a dart from the rack by the throw line and stand behind the line. Raise the dart in front of your eye: a red dot on the board shows where it will land. Let go of grip (or pull the trigger) to throw; a harder swing throws faster. The buttons under the slate change the game and double-out
  • Clays: press the red button on the thrower, or grab a clay off its stack and throw it out to sea for your crewmates to shoot
  • Rigging and crow's nest: grip the net behind the main mast and pull yourself up hand over hand (let go and you hang on). At the top press A/X to climb into the crow's nest; A/X again climbs back out. Keyboard: Space / Q at the net climbs up / down, R for the nest
  • Sailing: the ship is under way. Take the stairs beside the cabin door up to the quarterdeck and grip the big wheel's rim or handles (one hand or two) and turn it: clockwise steers to starboard (right), anticlockwise to port. It stays where you leave it. Near the shore or the other ship, the crew brings her about
  • Polly: the parrot on her perch by the wheel. Grab a cracker from the pack on the table and toss it up: she catches it and eats it on her perch. Hold your hand on her to pet her: she nuzzles in and coos
  • Cannons: take a match from the box on the crate by the net, pull the trigger to strike it (or drag it across the box), and touch the flame to the hole on top of a cannon's back end. Stand clear!
  • Scuba gear: tank: let go of it over your shoulder · mask: hold it to your face · fins: grab them
  • Swim: hold grip and pull your arms through the water
  • Bubble jets: trigger shoots bubbles from that hand and pushes you the other way; both hands for a boost, opposite directions to spin
  • Underwater sticks: left drifts where you look · right turns, up/down to rise and sink
  • Backpack: A/X, or grip over your shoulder, opens it; let go of an item over it (or over your shoulder) to stow it; grip a slot to take one out
  • Treasure map: click the left thumbstick to hold it up; turn it over for notes; grip it with the other hand and pull to zoom
  • Class skill (B): Navigator lights a trail to the next clue and reads hidden ink · Strongman heaves heavy things · Deep Diver shares air (and has double) · Fish Whisperer sends fish to fetch treasure. Pick a class on the crew board by the main mast
  • Magic (Y): collect tide runes, press Y, hold the trigger and draw a circle (Light Orb), triangle (Air Bubble) or zigzag (Current), then let go
  • Bots: empty slots are filled by bot divers. Point at one and pull the trigger to tell it to come, go somewhere, or use its skill
  • Crew: up to four divers; voices come from each diver and are muffled underwater. Touch the dive computer on your left wrist with your other hand to mute
  • Air: watch your left wrist; refill in the bubbling vent, grab the yellow tank, or swim up and put your head out of the water for a second
  • Desktop: drag to look, WASD move, Space jump/rise, Q sink, Shift jets; your hand: E grab/drop, F trigger, R reload / backpack, B skill, M map, 1/2/3 spells
