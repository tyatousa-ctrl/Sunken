Sunken Sicily

Co-op VR scuba treasure hunt · movement sandbox

Controls
  • Swim: hold grip and pull your arms through the water
  • Bubble jets: trigger shoots bubbles from that hand and pushes you the other way; both hands for a boost, opposite directions to spin
  • Left stick: drift where you look · right stick: turn, up/down to rise and sink
  • Grab: grip near an item (it glows), a rock or the sand to pull yourself along
  • Air: watch your left wrist; refill in the bubbling vent or grab the yellow tank
  • Desktop: drag to look, WASD drift, Q/E sink/rise, Shift jets
